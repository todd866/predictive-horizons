Connectome-only simulations carry environmental information but cannot coordinate motor output
Field coupling (ephaptic, neuropeptide, proprioceptive) rescues motor coordination on the real C. elegans connectome
The coordination advantage appears only at criticality — the synchronization phase transition
Low-dimensional observers systematically underestimate information in high-dimensional synchronized systems
Coding destroys ~85% of predictive content: intelligence lives upstream of code, in the coupling dynamics
