Biological prediction arises from organism-environment synchronization outpacing explicit measurement
Three timescales define the predictive regime: sync time, environmental autocorrelation, and measurement commitment time
Coding destroys most predictive content: simulation shows ~85% of future mutual information is lost at the commit boundary
Low-dimensional observers systematically underestimate the information in high-dimensional synchronized systems
Intelligence and prediction live upstream of code, in the oscillatory dynamics, not in the coded outputs
